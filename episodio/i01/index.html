Three meals, one of them the anchor

Desayuno runs morning, comida owns early-to-mid afternoon and is treated as the main event, and cena is later and usually lighter. None of this is a rule enforced anywhere — it's a pattern, and it varies by city and household.

An empty room is data, not rejection

A restaurant lit up and staffed but empty at six is not failing — it is simply between its two real rushes. Ask when la comida runs if you want the fuller room.

Tourist hours exist everywhere they need to

Hotel restaurants, airport counters and heavily touristed strips often serve on a schedule closer to what visitors expect. That is a legitimate choice for those places, not a trick — it just isn't the same clock as the neighbourhood fonda.
