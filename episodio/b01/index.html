📚 Going deeper — The Mexican sound model used in this course

The detail, if you want it. Entirely optional — the course works without it.

S, Z and soft C converge

Mexican Spanish is seseante: gracias, cinco and zapato use /s/. Do not import the th model from central Spain. This is a destination choice, not a simplified learner option.

gracias — GRAH-syahs
cincuenta — seen-KWEN-tah

Five vowels and one stress

Keep a, e, i, o and u stable, and give each word one clear stressed syllable. Accent marks override the default stress rules.

México — MEH-ksee-koh
hotel — oh-TEL
reservación — reh-sehr-bah-SYOHN

LL and Y share a lane

The Mexican model in this course is yeísta. For an English-speaking beginner, the Y in yes is the safe starting anchor. Regional realisations belong in receptive training later, not in your first production drill.

ayuda — ah-YOO-dah
llave — YAH-beh

Permission to sound foreign

A weak trill is not a travel emergency. Stable vowels, preserved syllables and correct stress buy more intelligibility than performing a regional accent. The target is understood first time, not mistaken for a local.
