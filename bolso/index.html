💡 Useful to know

  • 🧾 Restaurants and bars must show prices clearly, in national currency and with tax included, and must give you a receipt.
  • 💛 A tip is voluntary: it cannot be demanded, and it cannot be added to your bill without your consent — check the total before you pay.
  • 💳 An extra charge for paying by card is treated as an improper practice, not a normal fee.
  • 🚕 At the airport, ask an identified staff member where the authorized transport is; for an app ride, look for the designated meeting point.
  • 🎫 There is no single national transport system: ticket, card and validation change from city to city, so ask before you queue.
